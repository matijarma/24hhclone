# 24 Hours of Happy

An unofficial recreation of *Pharrell Williams — Happy (24 Hours of Happy)*, synced to your **local clock**. Whatever time it is for you, that's the hour you'll see.

## Make it yours

- `Customize` — paint your own day from fan videos filmed around the world. Pick a place, then brush it onto any 4-minute slot.
- `Randomize` — fill the whole day from whatever scope you've picked.
- `Shuffle` — sit back and let it pick for you, never repeating a clip until they're all played.

## Tips

- Hover anywhere to reveal the controls; they fade when you go idle.
- Click the center clock readout to jump back to now (same as `N`).
- Press `Space` to play or pause, `N` to resync to now.
- Double-tap for the fullscreen clock.
